"Opportunities that present themselves to you are the consequence -- at least partially -- of being in the _right place at the right time_. They tend to present themselves when you're _not expecting it_ \-- and often when you are engaged in other activities that would seem to preclude you from pursuing them. And they _come and go quickly_ \-- if you don't jump all over an opportunity, someone else generally will and it will vanish."
